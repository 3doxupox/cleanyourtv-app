Thank you, testers

These people tried test builds of the app on their own TVs and told us how it went. Every report made the app safer for everyone with the same TV. Tap anyone to see what they did.

The TV lists themselves come from Clean Your TV by Gathin and its community. This page thanks the people who tested this phone app.
