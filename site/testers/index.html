Thank you, testers

These people tried test builds of the app on their own TVs and told us how it went. Every report, the good ones and the ones where something did not work, made the app safer for everyone with the same TV.

The TV lists themselves come from Clean Your TV by Gathin and its community. This page thanks the people who tested this phone app.

Want to help?

If your TV has no tested list yet, open an issue with the app's TV report (in the app: Manage, then Help add this TV) and tick that you can test. We will point you to a test build when there is a list for your TV.

If you are on this list and want a change, or would rather not be listed, open an issue or comment on yours and we will update it the same day.
